# WithCare — Company Knowledge Base

## 1. General Product Information

**Q: When was WithCare created?**
A: WithCare was founded in 2024.

**Q: What can I do in WithCare?**
A: WithCare gives you one place to manage the most important parts of caregiving. You can organize tasks, events, contacts, notes, behavior logs, and medications, bring family and friends together in a care circle, make requests for help, and rely on the Navigator for support when you need it.

**Q: What does the Navigator do?**
A: The Navigator is your virtual care concierge. It can help complete tasks, answer questions, suggest resources, and provide guidance based on caregiving best practices. Think of it as a supportive teammate that's always available to help lighten your load.

**Q: What is a care circle?**
A: A care circle is your private group of family, friends, and helpers who support a care recipient. It keeps everyone connected and updated.

## 2. Account & Access

**Q: How do I reset my WithCare password?**
A: On the login page, select "Forgot password" and follow the instructions to reset it.

**Q: How do I log back in if I forgot my username/email?**
A: Please reach out to hello@joinwithcare.com so we can securely help you recover access.

**Q: How can I delete my WithCare account?**
A: Go to your profile settings and select "Delete account." This will permanently remove your data from our system.

## 3. Care Circle & Collaboration

**Q: How do I invite someone to my care circle?**
A: In the "Updates" tab, select the option to invite others to your care circle.

**Q: How do I remove someone from my care circle?**
A: When viewing your care circle members in the Updates tab, there is an option to remove members. Only the primary caregiver and care recipient can remove members.

**Q: Can I be in more than one care circle?**
A: Yes! You can join multiple care circles if you're helping care for more than one person.

**Q: Can I add more than one care recipient?**
A: Yes! You can add as many care recipients as you need.

**Q: Can multiple family members use one account?**
A: We recommend each family member create their own account so it's easier to track who sent updates and who is assigned to tasks.

**Q: What happens if two caregivers manage the same recipient?**
A: Whoever added the care recipient and started the care circle is the primary caregiver in-app. They can invite other caregivers to join.

**Q: How do I leave a care circle?**
A: If you no longer want to be part of a care circle, please ask the primary caregiver or care recipient to remove you.

**Q: How do I set permissions for care circle members?**
A: When you invite someone to a care circle, you can determine the level of access they should have when they join.
